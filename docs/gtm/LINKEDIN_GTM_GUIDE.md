## Daily Routine (30 min total)

### Morning (15 min) - Prospecting & Outreach

**1. Find 15-20 Prospects (5 min)**

Use LinkedIn Sales Navigator or Boolean search:

**Executive Search:**
```
(CEO OR "Chief Executive Officer" OR CHRO OR "VP Talent")
AND ("Series B" OR "Series C" OR "growth stage")
AND (Technology OR Software OR SaaS)
```

**AI Ops:**
```
(CTO OR "Chief Technology Officer" OR "VP Engineering")
AND (AI OR "machine learning" OR "artificial intelligence" OR LLM)
AND (Software OR Technology OR SaaS)
```

**IDRE:**
```
(CFO OR "Chief Financial Officer" OR COO OR "VP Revenue Cycle")
AND ("health system" OR hospital OR "medical center")
AND "United States"
```

**2. Send 15 Connection Requests (10 min)**

Click "Connect" → "Add a note"

**Templates (pick one, personalize):**

**Executive Search:**
> Hi {{FirstName}}, saw {{Company}}'s growth. We place C-suite executives in 2 weeks vs 6 months. Worth connecting?

**AI Ops:**
> Hi {{FirstName}}, saw {{Company}}'s AI work. We do 24/7 AI ops so systems never fail. Worth connecting?

**IDRE:**
> Hi {{FirstName}}, helping health systems with No Surprises Act compliance at $400-600/determination. Worth connecting?

**Pro Tips:**
- Mention a specific signal (funding round, product launch, LinkedIn post)
- Keep it under 280 characters (LinkedIn limit is 300)
- End with a question

## LinkedIn Best Practices

**Profile Optimization:**
- Headline: "Building Wells Onyx | Executive Search, AI Operations, Healthcare Compliance"
- Banner: Wells Onyx branding
- Featured: Case studies, testimonials, recent placements
- Activity: Post 2-3x/week about industry insights

**Engagement Strategy:**
- Like 5-10 prospect posts daily (before sending connection request)
- Comment thoughtfully on 2-3 posts (build familiarity)
- Share relevant content (AI trends, exec search insights, healthcare compliance)

**Warm-up Tactics:**
- Check "Who viewed your profile" → Send connection request to warm leads
- Join relevant groups (SaaS CEOs, AI/ML Engineers, Healthcare Finance)
- Engage in groups before reaching out 1:1

**LinkedIn Limits:**
- 100 connection requests/week (max)
- 250 connection requests pending at once
- Unlimited messages to connections
- No hard limit on profile views

## Signal Detection

**What to look for before reaching out:**

**Executive Search:**
- Recent funding announcement (check Crunchbase, TechCrunch)
- "We're hiring!" posts on LinkedIn
- 10+ open positions on careers page
- Recent exec departure (check LinkedIn job changes)

**AI Ops:**
- Recent AI product launch
- Hiring ML engineers (check job posts)
- Posted about AI/ML on LinkedIn
- Company profile mentions AI/ML

**IDRE:**
- Hospital acquisitions (integration = IDR pain)
- Posted about revenue cycle challenges
- Large health system (5,000+ employees)
- Multiple facilities = high out-of-network volume
