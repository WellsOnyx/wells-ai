# Wells HR - Complete Employment System

## Executive Summary

Wells HR enables flexible employment arrangements for independent professionals working through Wells AI/Wells Onyx. The system supports 5 different employment models - from W2 with full benefits to Corp-to-Corp arrangements - allowing professionals to choose what fits their situation while protecting Wells Onyx from employment risk.

**Key Innovation:** Built with integrity - even competitors can use Wells HR to manage their contractors through Wells AI platform.

---

## System Overview

### The Problem

Independent professionals working through B2B platforms face a dilemma:
- **W2 Employment:** Stable with benefits, but restricts flexibility and creates unemployment liability
- **1099 Contractor:** Maximum flexibility, but complicated taxes and no benefits
- **No good middle ground**

### The Solution

Wells HR offers **5 employment models** on a spectrum from full employment to pure contractor:

1. **W2 with Benefits** - Full employee, health insurance, 401k, PTO
2. **W2 Contractor** - W2 tax status but no benefits (middle ground)
3. **1099 Contractor** - Traditional independent contractor
4. **Corp-to-Corp (C2C)** - Contractor's LLC/Corp contracts with Wells
5. **EOR (Employer of Record)** - Third-party employs worker, contracts with Wells

Each model has different tradeoffs in:
- Benefits (health insurance, 401k, PTO)
- Tax handling (employer withholds vs contractor responsible)
- Unemployment eligibility
- Legal protection
- Cost structure

## The 5 Employment Models

### 1. W2 with Benefits

**Best For:** Full-time professionals (30+ hrs/week), long-term relationships

**Structure:**
- Full employee status with all legal protections
- Health insurance (employer contributes 50-75%)
- 401k with 3-4% match
- 15 days PTO per year
- Workers comp coverage
- Tax withholding automatic

**Costs:**
- Platform fee: 5% of gross
- Payroll fee: $49/month
- Benefits fee: $400-800/month
- Employer taxes: 7.65% (FICA)
- Workers comp: 1-3%
- Unemployment insurance: varies by state

**Total overhead:** ~18% above base salary

**Risks:**
- Unemployment eligible (worker can claim if terminated)
- Must follow strict employment laws
- Harder to terminate quickly
- Most expensive option

**When to Use:**
- Long-term core team members
- Full-time professionals with families needing health insurance
- Roles requiring consistent 30+ hours/week

---

### 2. W2 Contractor (No Benefits)

**Best For:** Part-time professionals (20-30 hrs/week), project-based work

**Structure:**
- W2 tax status (employer withholds taxes)
- No health insurance, 401k, or PTO
- Workers comp covered
- Still eligible for unemployment (risk to company)
- Lower cost than full benefits

**Costs:**
- Platform fee: 5% of gross
- Payroll fee: $29/month
- Employer taxes: 7.65%
- Workers comp: 1-3%
- Unemployment insurance: varies

**Total overhead:** ~11% above base salary

**Risks:**
- Still eligible for unemployment despite waiver attempts
- Some state courts invalidate unemployment waivers

**Mitigation Strategy:**
Use **Modified W2 Agreement** with:
- Independent contractor classification language
- No expectation of continued work clause
- Unemployment waiver (may not be enforceable in all states)
- At-will termination
- Project-based engagement terms

**When to Use:**
- Part-time regular contributors
- Project-based work with regular income
- Workers who have benefits elsewhere (spouse's plan, VA, Medicare)
- Want tax simplicity (no quarterly estimated taxes)

---

### 3. 1099 Independent Contractor

**Best For:** Experienced freelancers, multiple income sources, flexibility-focused

**Structure:**
- True independent contractor
- Contractor handles all taxes quarterly
- No benefits, no workers comp
- Not eligible for unemployment
- Lowest platform fees

**Costs:**
- Platform fee: 3.3% of gross (33bps Wells AI fee only)
- No payroll fees
- No benefits fees

**Total overhead:** 3.3% above contract value

**Worker Responsibilities:**
- Quarterly estimated taxes (federal + state)
- Self-employment tax (15.3% of net income)
- Own health insurance
- Own retirement savings
- Business expense tracking
- Own equipment/tools

**Risks (Misclassification):**
- Must pass IRS 20-Factor Test for IC classification
- Cannot control how work is done
- Cannot require set hours or location
- Cannot provide extensive training
- Worker must be able to work for others simultaneously

**When to Use:**
- Experienced independent contractors with LLC/S-Corp
- Part-time/freelance work
- Multiple simultaneous clients
- Short-term projects

---

### 4. Corp-to-Corp (C2C)

**Best For:** Established businesses, agencies, high-earning consultants

**Structure:**
- Contractor's LLC/Corp contracts with Wells Onyx
- No employment relationship whatsoever
- Cleanest legal separation
- Contractor can hire subcontractors under their entity
- Maximum liability protection

**Costs:**
- Platform fee: 3.3% of gross
- No payroll fees
- No benefits fees

**Total overhead:** 3.3% above contract value

**Contractor Requirements:**
- Must have existing LLC/S-Corp/C-Corp
- General liability insurance (GL)
- Professional liability insurance (E&O)
- Business bank account
- Invoice on entity letterhead
- W-9 from entity (not individual)

**Risks:**
- None for Wells Onyx (no employment relationship)
- Contractor bears all business risk

**When to Use:**
- Agencies/firms with multiple people
- High-earning professionals ($200K+/year)
- Long-term established consultants
- Anyone with existing business entity

---

### 5. EOR (Employer of Record)

**Best For:** International contractors, compliance-heavy situations, temporary workers

**Structure:**
- Third-party company (Deel, Remote, Oyster) employs worker
- EOR handles all HR, payroll, benefits, compliance
- EOR contracts with Wells Onyx for services
- Worker is employee of EOR, not Wells Onyx

**Costs:**
- Platform fee: 3.3% to Wells AI
- EOR fee: $599/month
- Benefits fee: $200-600/month (from EOR)

**Total overhead:** $800-1,200/month + 3.3% platform fee

**Benefits:**
- International workers (any country)
- Compliance handled by EOR
- Unemployment risk transferred to EOR
- Benefits available without Wells Onyx being employer
- Quick setup (1-2 days)

**Recommended EOR Providers:**
- **Deel** - 150+ countries, $599/month, fast setup
- **Remote.com** - 70+ countries, $599/month, great support
- **Oyster HR** - 180+ countries, $599/month, equity grants
- **Justworks** - US only, $59-99/month, PEO model
- **Rippling** - US + select international, $35/month base

**When to Use:**
- International contractors (outside US)
- Workers in strict employment law states (CA, NY, MA)
- Compliance-heavy industries (finance, healthcare)
- 3-12 month temporary engagements

## Legal Risk Mitigation

### Modified W2 Agreement (Unemployment Waiver)

**For Model 2 (W2 Contractor)** - attempt to reduce unemployment risk:

**Key Clauses:**

1. **IC Classification Language**
```
Although Contractor will be issued a W-2 for tax reporting purposes,
this arrangement is solely for the convenience of tax withholding and
does not create an employer-employee relationship as defined by state
unemployment insurance laws.
```

2. **Contractor Acknowledgments**
- This is a contract for services, not employment
- No expectation of continued work beyond current project
- Free to work for other clients simultaneously
- Sets own schedule and methods
- No direct supervision
- Provides own tools/equipment where practical
- Engaged for specific deliverables, not time-based employment

3. **Unemployment Waiver**
```
Contractor expressly waives any right to claim unemployment compensation
benefits from Company upon termination of this Agreement.
```

**WARNING:** This waiver may not be enforceable in many states.

**State-by-State Enforceability:**

**Enforceable (24 states):**
TX, FL, GA, TN, AZ, NC, SC, IN, OH, MO, AL, MS, LA, AR, OK, KS, NE, SD, ND, WY, ID, UT, NV, AK

**Limited Enforceability (18 states):**
PA, MI, WI, MN, IA, KY, WV, VA, NH, ME, VT, DE, MD, CO, NM, OR, WA, MT

**Not Enforceable (8 states + DC):**
CA, NY, NJ, MA, CT, RI, IL, HI, DC

**State-Specific Notes:**
- **California:** Does not allow unemployment waivers. Must use true IC (Model 3) or EOR (Model 5).
- **New York:** Heavily scrutinizes IC classification. EOR recommended.
- **New Jersey:** Presumes employment unless proven otherwise.
- **Massachusetts:** ABC test makes IC classification very difficult.
- **Illinois:** Strict IC rules. Waiver likely unenforceable.

## IRS 20-Factor Test (IC vs Employee)

**For Model 3 (1099) to be valid, must pass this test:**

| Factor | Employee | Independent Contractor |
|--------|----------|------------------------|
| Instructions | Company controls how work is done | Contractor decides how |
| Training | Company provides training | Contractor already skilled |
| Integration | Work integral to business | Separate from business operations |
| Services Rendered Personally | Must be done by worker | Can hire others |
| Hiring Assistants | Company hires | Contractor hires |
| Continuing Relationship | Ongoing work | Project-based |
| Set Hours | Company dictates schedule | Contractor sets hours |
| Full-Time Required | Must work full-time | Can work for others |
| Location | At company premises | Contractor chooses |
| Order/Sequence | Company controls | Contractor decides |
| Reports | Regular reports required | Only deliverables |
| Payment Method | By hour/week/month | By project/job |
| Expenses | Company reimburses | Contractor pays own |
| Tools/Materials | Company provides | Contractor provides |
| Investment | None | Invests in own facilities |
| Profit/Loss | No risk | Can profit or lose |
| Multiple Clients | Exclusive to one | Works for several |
| Services to Public | No | Offers to general public |
| Discharge | Can be fired | Contract termination only |
| Termination | Can quit anytime | Liable for non-completion |

**Scoring:**
- **0-5 factors point to Employee:** Safe for IC
- **6-10 factors:** Borderline (use C2C or EOR)
- **11+ factors:** Must be W2 employee

## Onboarding Flow

### User Experience

1. **Professional visits Wells AI HR**
   - Sees all 5 models side-by-side
   - Clear pros/cons for each
   - Cost breakdown
   - Risk profiles

2. **System recommends model based on:**
   - Work type (full-time vs part-time vs project)
   - Location (state compliance requirements)
   - Benefits needs
   - Existing business entity (LLC/Corp)

3. **Professional selects model**
   - Clicks "Select This Model"

4. **System collects information**
   - Full name, email, address
   - SSN (for W2/1099) or EIN (for C2C)
   - Entity details if C2C
   - Any other model-specific requirements

5. **System generates agreement**
   - Custom agreement based on selected model
   - Shows preview in browser
   - Sends via DocuSign for signature

6. **Professional signs**
   - DocuSign e-signature
   - Automatic notification to HR
   - Record updated in Notion

7. **Status updated → Active**
   - Professional can start accepting work
   - Payroll integration activated (if W2)
   - Billing integration activated (if 1099/C2C)

**Timeline:** 1-3 days (vs weeks with traditional HR)

## Notion Database Schema

### HR Employees Database

**Properties:**

- **Name** (Title) - Full legal name
- **Email** (Email) - Primary contact
- **Employment Model** (Select) - W2 with Benefits, W2 Contractor, 1099, C2C, EOR
- **Legal Structure** (Select) - W2, 1099, C2C, EOR
- **Status** (Select) - 📝 Pending Signature, ✅ Active, ⏸️ Paused, ❌ Terminated
- **Address** (Text) - Full mailing address
- **City** (Text)
- **State** (Select) - All 50 states + DC
- **ZIP** (Text)
- **SSN (Last 4)** (Text) - Last 4 digits only
- **Entity Name** (Text) - For C2C: LLC/Corp name
- **EIN** (Text) - For C2C: Business EIN
- **Platform Fee** (Text) - Fee structure (e.g., "5% of gross")
- **Payroll Fee** (Text) - Monthly payroll processing fee
- **Benefits Included** (Checkbox) - Whether employee gets benefits
- **Unemployment Eligible** (Checkbox) - Whether eligible for unemployment
- **Tax Withholding** (Select) - Employer Withholds, Contractor Responsible, EOR Handles
- **Start Date** (Date) - Employment start date
- **End Date** (Date) - If terminated
- **YTD Gross Pay** (Number, Currency) - Year-to-date gross compensation
- **YTD Taxes Withheld** (Number, Currency) - YTD tax withholding (W2 only)
- **YTD Net Pay** (Number, Currency) - YTD net paid to worker
- **Contract Signed** (Checkbox) - Whether employment agreement signed
- **DocuSign Envelope ID** (Text) - DocuSign envelope ID
- **I-9 Verified** (Checkbox) - I-9 completed (W2 only)
- **W-4 on File** (Checkbox) - W-4 on file (W2 only)
- **W-9 on File** (Checkbox) - W-9 on file (1099/C2C only)
- **Insurance Certificate** (Files) - For C2C: GL and E&O insurance
- **Notes** (Text) - Internal notes
- **Created** (Created Time) - Auto-timestamp
- **Last Updated** (Last Edited Time) - Auto-timestamp

## Integration Points

### DocuSign (Agreement Signing)

**Templates Needed:**
1. W2 with Benefits Employment Agreement
2. W2 Contractor Agreement (with unemployment waiver)
3. 1099 Independent Contractor Agreement
4. Corp-to-Corp Master Services Agreement
5. EOR Pass-Through Agreement

**Workflow:**
1. Professional selects model and completes info
2. System generates DocuSign envelope
3. Sends to professional's email
4. Professional signs
5. Webhook notifies Wells HR
6. Status updated to ✅ Active
7. PDF attached to Notion record

### Payroll Systems

**For W2 Employees (Models 1 & 2):**

**Recommended:** Gusto or Rippling

**Integration:**
1. When Status = ✅ Active and Model = W2
2. Sync to payroll system
3. Set up recurring payroll schedule (bi-weekly or monthly)
4. Configure tax withholding from W-4
5. Set up benefits if Model 1
6. Sync gross pay back to Notion monthly

**For 1099/C2C (Models 3 & 4):**

**Recommended:** Bill.com or QuickBooks

**Integration:**
1. When invoice received from contractor
2. Create bill in Bill.com
3. Route for approval
4. Pay via ACH
5. Update YTD Gross Pay in Notion
6. Generate 1099-NEC at year-end from YTD data

**For EOR (Model 5):**

**Workflow:**
1. Wells Onyx contracts with EOR
2. EOR employs worker
3. Wells Onyx pays EOR monthly invoice
4. EOR handles all payroll/benefits
5. Update YTD data in Notion from EOR reports
