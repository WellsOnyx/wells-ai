# How to Establish IDRE Credibility Without Naming Clients

## The Challenge
You work with 2 of the 15 CMS-approved IDRE agencies, but you can't name them publicly.

## The Solution
Use aggregate metrics, proof points, and conditional references instead.

---

## What to SAY (Generic Proof)

### In First Outreach:
```
"We work with 2 of the 15 CMS-approved IDRE agencies"
```
- ✅ Shows you're in the market
- ✅ Doesn't violate confidentiality
- ✅ Creates curiosity

### In Discovery Call:
```
"We process 5,000+ IDR determinations per month across our agency partners"
```
- ✅ Shows scale
- ✅ Aggregate data (not client-specific)
- ✅ Demonstrates capability

### When They Ask for References:
```
"Happy to provide references once we're further along in discussions. Our current partners are confidential, but I can share:
• Our quality metrics (≥98% accuracy)
• Our capacity (900+ determinations/day)
• Anonymous case studies"
```
- ✅ Professional approach
- ✅ Shows you respect confidentiality
- ✅ Builds trust (they know you'll protect their info too)

---

## What to SHARE (Proof Without Names)

### 1. Aggregate Metrics
```
"Across our IDRE agency partners:
• 5,000+ determinations/month
• ≥98% quality (virtually zero CMS reversals)
• $450/determination avg cost
• 30 determinations/day per Practice Lead
• 2+ years operating in IDRE space"
```

### 2. Anonymous Case Studies
```
"One of our agency partners came to us with:
• Challenge: Processing 300 determinations/month at $800 each
• Our Solution: Scaled to 900/month at $450 each
• Result: $2.4M annual savings, 3x capacity increase
• Timeline: 60 days to full scale"
```

### 3. Process Documentation
```
"Here's our IDRE operations playbook:
• 7-step determination process
• Quality assurance checklist
• CMS compliance framework
• Audit trail documentation"
```
- Shows you know the business
- Doesn't require client names
- Demonstrates expertise

### 4. Certifications & Partnerships
```
"Our Practice Leads are:
• Certified in No Surprises Act compliance
• Trained in IDR determination protocols
• Experienced with all major payer types
• Licensed across all 50 states"
```

## When They INSIST on References

### Phase 1: First Meeting
**Say:**
```
"I respect that you need references. Here's my process:

1. Let's do a discovery call (understand your needs)
2. I'll share anonymous case studies
3. If there's a fit, we'll do a pilot (30 days, 100 determinations)
4. If the pilot goes well, I'll introduce you to a current partner

This protects their confidentiality and gives you proof through results."
```

### Phase 2: After Pilot
**When pilot succeeds, then ask current clients:**
```
"[Client A], would you be comfortable serving as a reference for a potential new partner? They're another IDRE agency evaluating our services."
```
- Get explicit permission first
- Only after pilot proves value
- Frame it as helping another agency (not just a sales reference)

## Positioning That Builds Trust

### "We're Protective of Client Confidentiality"
```
"You'll notice I'm careful not to name our current partners publicly. That's intentional.

When you work with us, I'll protect your confidentiality the same way.

Some agencies want to be public about our partnership (great for both of us), others prefer to keep it quiet (we honor that)."
```
- ✅ Turns limitation into strength
- ✅ Shows professionalism
- ✅ Builds trust

### "Let Our Work Speak"
```
"Rather than name-drop, I'd rather prove our value:

1. Free consultation (1 hour) - review your current operations
2. Custom proposal - tailored to your specific needs
3. Pilot program (30 days, 100 determinations) - prove we can deliver
4. Then you decide if it's a fit

No references needed until you've seen our work firsthand."
```
- ✅ Action-oriented
- ✅ Low-risk for prospect
- ✅ Confidence in your work

## The "Later Reference" Strategy

### During Pilot Program
**Build relationship, then ask:**
```
"This pilot is going really well. When we move to full partnership, would you be open to serving as a reference for future prospects?

You'd get:
• Priority support (you're helping us grow)
• Preferential pricing (loyalty discount)
• Early access to new capabilities

And we'd only share:
• Your role/company (with permission)
• High-level results (no confidential data)
• Your testimonial (your words, your approval)"
```

### After 6+ Months
**They're happy, you've proven value:**
```
"You've been a great partner for 6 months. We're talking to [New Agency] about our services.

Would you be comfortable taking a 15-min reference call? I'll brief them on our work together (with your approval on what I share).

Happy to reciprocate however I can help [Your Agency]."
```

## What NEVER to Share (Client Confidentiality)

❌ Client names (without permission)
❌ Client-specific volume data
❌ Client-specific pricing
❌ Client internal challenges
❌ Client relationships with payers
❌ Client competitive information

## Template Responses to Common Questions

### "Who are your current clients?"
```
"I work with 2 of the 15 CMS-approved IDRE agencies. They prefer to keep our partnership confidential, and I respect that.

What I can share is our aggregate performance: 5,000+ determinations/month, ≥98% quality, $450/determination.

Once we're further along, happy to provide references with their permission."
```

### "Can you provide a reference?"
```
"Yes, but I'd like to earn your trust through our work first:

1. Let me do a free operational review (1 hour)
2. Share our pilot program structure (30 days, 100 determinations)
3. If you like what you see, we'll do the pilot
4. If the pilot succeeds, I'll introduce you to a current partner

This way you get proof through results, not just a phone call."
```

### "How do I know you're legitimate?"
```
"Fair question. Here's how:

1. Our metrics: ≥98% quality, 900+/day capacity, 2+ years in IDRE
2. Our process: I'll walk you through our 7-step determination framework
3. Our offer: 30-day pilot with money-back guarantee
4. Our references: Available after pilot (with client permission)

I'm confident our work will speak for itself."
```

## Key Principle

**Client confidentiality = Your competitive advantage**

When prospects see you protect current clients, they trust you'll protect them too.

This builds MORE trust than name-dropping ever could.

---

**Remember:**
- References come AFTER pilot, not before
- Aggregate data > client names
- Your work speaks louder than testimonials
- Confidentiality = professionalism
