## Strategy 2: IDRE Whale Hunt

### The Market

**Total Market:** Only 15 CMS-approved IDRE agencies

**Target:** 3 more agencies (33% total market share)
**Value:** Each agency = $10M-$100M+ annual run-rate

### Why This is Different

**NOT** a volume play. **NOT** partner ecosystem distribution.

**IS** a relationship-based, multi-month, white-glove sales process.

### The Whale Hunt Process

**Phase 1: Research & Intelligence (30 days)**
- Pull CMS-approved agency list (public data)
- Research each agency (leadership, volume, pain points)
- Identify warm intro paths
- Create intel dossiers on top 3 targets

**Phase 2: Warm Introduction (30-45 days)**
- Never cold outreach
- Paths: Hospital CFO intros, industry conferences, mutual connections
- Secure first meetings with agency CEOs

**Phase 3: Executive Sponsorship Campaign (90 days)**
- Week 1: Discovery meeting (learn their business)
- Week 2: Custom proposal (tailored to their needs)
- Week 4: Pilot program (30 days, 100 determinations)
- Week 8: Results presentation (prove value)
- Week 12: Full partnership negotiation

**Phase 4: Account Management (Ongoing)**
- Monthly check-ins
- Quarterly business reviews
- Continuous improvement
- Upsell additional services
