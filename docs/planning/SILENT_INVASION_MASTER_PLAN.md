## The 4-Tier System

### TIER 1: Omnipresence (Before They Know Your Name)

**Goal:** Be everywhere they already are

**LinkedIn:**
- Post 3x/week: Case studies, insights, data (no pitches)
- Comment on 20 prospect posts/day (thoughtful, not salesy)
- Like 50 prospect posts/day
- Connect to their connections (build mutual network)

**Twitter (for AI Ops):**
- Tweet 5x/day: Technical insights, AI ops lessons
- Reply to 20 CTO tweets/day (helpful, technical)
- Share AI incident learnings

**Content Assets:**
- "48-Hour Executive Search Framework" (PDF)
- "AI Operations Incident Response Checklist" (Notion template)
- "No Surprises Act ROI Calculator" (interactive tool)

**Result:** When you reach out, they've seen your name 10-20 times already

---

### TIER 2: Warm Introduction Path (Before Direct Outreach)

**Goal:** Never cold message. Always have context.

**Mutual Connections:**
- Clay enriches with: Who do we both know?
- Connect to their investors, board members, advisors first
- When you reach out: "I see we both know {{MutualConnection}}"

**Engagement First:**
- Engage with their content 3-5 times
- THEN send connection request
- Reference their post in connection note

**Example:**
> Hi {{FirstName}}, saw your post about {{Topic}} - spot on. We help companies like {{Company}} with {{Solution}}. Worth connecting?

**Result:** 3-5x higher acceptance rate than cold requests

### TIER 4: Timing-Based Triggers (Strike When Iron is Hot)

**Goal:** Don't reach out randomly. Reach out when they NEED you.

**Executive Search Triggers:**

| Trigger | Timing | Message Angle | Why It Works |
|---------|--------|---------------|--------------|
| Funding announcement | 7-14 days after | "Congrats on the raise. Probably thinking about leadership now..." | Companies hire execs 30-90 days after raising money |
| Executive departure | Within 48 hours | "Saw {{Exec}} left. If you need a {{Role}}..." | Urgent need = fast decision |
| Hiring surge | Immediately | "10+ new roles. Need leadership to manage the growth?" | Rapid scaling = need for senior talent |

**AI Ops Triggers:**

| Trigger | Timing | Message Angle | Why It Works |
|---------|--------|---------------|--------------|
| AI product launch | 30 days after | "Congrats on {{Feature}}. 30 days in, ops pain usually hits..." | Production issues emerge 30-60 days post-launch |
| AI incident | Within 24 hours | "Saw the {{Issue}}. We handle 24/7 ops if you need backup..." | Pain is fresh = high urgency |
| Hiring ML engineers | Immediately | "Hiring ML team = who's running ops?" | New AI initiative = need for ops |

**IDRE Triggers:**

| Trigger | Timing | Message Angle | Why It Works |
|---------|--------|---------------|--------------|
| Hospital acquisition | 60 days after | "Integration means 3x the IDR volume. We can help..." | Acquisitions = immediate scale need |
| CMS audit | Within 7 days | "Saw audit findings. We can help with remediation..." | Audit = urgent compliance need |
| Revenue cycle hiring | Immediately | "Hiring for IDR leadership = scaling pain. We're your outsourced team..." | Hiring = they know they have a problem |

**Result:** You reach out when they're actively feeling the pain

- Use: Instantly.ai (50+ warmed domains)
- Volume: 100-150/day across all domains
- Sequences: 3-touch (Day 0, Day 4, Day 10)

### LinkedIn (If Available) - Channel Priority #2
- Engage first (like/comment 3-5 times)
- Then: Connection request (personalized)
- Once connected: Message sequence
- Limit: 100 connection requests/week

### Twitter (For AI Ops Only) - Channel Priority #3
- Engage first (reply to 3-5 tweets)
- Then: DM (if they follow back)
- Public engagement builds credibility

### SMS (INVITATION ONLY) - Channel Priority #4
- **NEVER** unsolicited
- **ONLY** after they reply to email/LinkedIn
- Ask permission: "Would you prefer to text? Here's my number: XXX"
- Use for: Hot leads, deal closing, urgent updates

## The Trust Equation

```
Trust = (Credibility + Reliability + Intimacy) / Self-Orientation
```

**Maximize:**
- Credibility: Social proof, case studies, thought leadership
- Reliability: Consistent presence, follow-through
- Intimacy: Personal connection, understand their pain

**Minimize:**
- Self-Orientation: Don't pitch, provide value first
