# Founder Journey: Your AI Co-Founder

Wells AI acts as your AI co-founder, helping you manage all aspects of running a startup - from hiring executives to finding customers to raising capital.

## Overview

Founders wear many hats. Wells proactively helps across all functions:

- **Hiring**: Use Wells Onyx to find C-level executives (CTO, CFO, CISO)
- **GTM**: Launch automated campaigns to find customers
- **Procurement**: Source suppliers, negotiate deals, use GRAIN for fast payments
- **Fundraising**: Draft pitch decks, connect with investors
- **Operations**: Build processes, set up tools, manage workflows

## Founder Journey Stages

Wells adapts its recommendations based on your company stage:

### Stage 0: Ideation
**Focus**: Validate product-market fit, build MVP

**What Wells Does**:
- Help you find early customers to validate your idea
- Source equipment/tools for MVP at startup rates
- Connect with potential co-founders

**Common Mistakes to Avoid**:
- Building in isolation without customer feedback
- Spending on expensive tools before validation
- Hiring too early

**Example Conversation**:
```
User: "I'm validating a B2B SaaS idea for procurement automation."

Wells: "Perfect timing to talk to potential customers before building.

Who would be your ideal first 10 customers? I can help you find and reach out to CFOs or procurement heads who'd give you honest feedback."
```

---

## Founder Intent Detection

Wells automatically detects when you're a founder and classifies your needs.

### Detection Signals

**Strong Signals** (high confidence):
- "I founded..."
- "I'm the CEO..."
- "We just raised..."
- "My startup..."
- "Pre-seed", "Seed round", "Series A"

**Medium Signals** (moderate confidence):
- "My company"
- "We need to hire"
- "Raising capital"
- "Need a CFO/CTO"
- "First customers"
- "Burn rate", "runway"

**Pattern Recognition**:
- Multiple pain points = likely founder
- E.g., "I need to hire AND find customers AND raise money"

### Context Extraction

Wells extracts key metrics from conversation:
- **Company name**: "I'm building FinanceOS"
- **Team size**: "We have 12 people"
- **Burn rate**: "Burning $125K/month"
- **Runway**: "18 months of runway"
- **Revenue**: "$1.2M ARR"
- **Last round**: "Series A"

## Founder-Specific Flows

### 1. Hiring Flow (Wells Onyx)

**What It Does**:
- Launch executive search for C-level roles
- Wells Onyx sources 50+ candidates
- AI screens for stage fit and culture match
- Presents top 3-5 candidates with profiles
- Helps negotiate offers

**Supported Roles**:
- CTO / VP Engineering
- CFO
- CISO / Head of Security
- VP Sales / CRO
- VP Product / CPO

**How to Start**:
```
User: "I need to hire a CFO for fundraising and unit economics."

Wells: "Wells Onyx executive search launched. I'll find CFOs with startup experience and proven fundraising track records.

In 2 weeks, I'll present 3-5 pre-qualified candidates. What's the biggest challenge your finance function is facing right now?"
```

**Timeline**: 2 weeks to top candidates

---

### 2. GTM Flow (Customer Acquisition)

**What It Does**:
- Analyzes your won deals to build ICP
- Finds 50+ prospects matching your ideal customer profile
- Generates personalized outreach (email, LinkedIn, SMS)
- Tracks responses and books meetings automatically
- Updates pipeline in your Notion

**How to Start**:
```
User: "Need to find 50 enterprise customers for our B2B platform."

Wells: "Perfect. I'll run a targeted GTM campaign.

First, tell me about your product - what problem does it solve and who gets the most value from it?"
```

**Requirements**:
- Notion workspace (or use Wells stack)
- Product description
- Target customer profile

**Timeline**: 1 week to first meetings

---

### 3. Procurement Flow (Sourcing & Negotiation)

**What It Does**:
- Sources suppliers from pre-vetted network
- Gets 3-5 competitive quotes within 48 hours
- Negotiates final terms
- Closes deals with GRAIN (1-2 day settlement)

**Common Requests**:
- Office space
- Cloud credits (AWS, GCP, Azure)
- Equipment and hardware
- Software tools
- Professional services

**How to Start**:
```
User: "Need to source office space in downtown SF, 5000 sq ft."

Wells: "RFQ created. I'm pinging pre-vetted landlords in the SF Financial District.

You'll have 3-5 quotes within 48 hours. What's your move-in timeline and monthly budget?"
```

**GRAIN Advantage**:
- Traditional: 30-45 day payment cycles
- GRAIN: 1-2 day settlement
- Earn 4.5% APY on treasury
- Get 1.5-2x credit lines

**Timeline**: 48 hours to quotes

---

### 4. Fundraising Flow (Capital Raising)

**What It Does**:
- Drafts data-backed pitch deck
- Identifies investors who fund your stage/industry
- Generates warm intro emails
- Tracks conversations in Notion
- Helps with term sheet negotiation

**How to Start**:
```
User: "Need to raise Series A. We're at $1.5M ARR growing 20% MoM."

Wells: "Strong position for Series A. Let's build your pitch.

What's your target raise amount, and what will you use it for (hiring, GTM, product)?"
```

**Deliverables**:
- 15-slide pitch deck
- Investor target list (15-20 matches)
- Warm intro emails
- Fundraising tracker

**Timeline**: 1 week to start conversations

## Integration Points

### Wells Onyx (Executive Search)
- **Purpose**: Find C-level talent
- **Timeline**: 2 weeks to top candidates
- **Roles**: CTO, CFO, CISO, VP Sales, VP Product
- **Process**: AI-powered sourcing → Screening → Top 3-5 matches → Interviews → Offers

### GTM Engine (Customer Acquisition)
- **Purpose**: Find and close customers
- **Timeline**: 1 week to first meetings
- **Channels**: Email, LinkedIn, SMS
- **Process**: ICP analysis → Prospect search → Outreach → Pipeline tracking

### RFQ System (Procurement)
- **Purpose**: Source suppliers and negotiate deals
- **Timeline**: 48 hours to quotes
- **Categories**: Office, equipment, cloud, services
- **Process**: RFQ → Supplier matching → Quotes → Negotiation → GRAIN settlement

### GRAIN Vault (Payments)
- **Purpose**: Fast B2B settlements and buying power
- **Benefits**: 1-2 day settlement (vs 30-45), 4.5% APY, 1.5-2x credit lines
- **Process**: Fund vault → Get credit line → Buy with instant settlement → Earn yield

### Notion Integration
- **Purpose**: Track everything in one place
- **Databases**: Deals, Hiring, Partnerships, RFQs, Investors
- **Sync**: Real-time updates from Wells
- **Access**: Via Wells chat or Slack commands

## Technical Implementation

### Founder Detection
**File**: `/lib/journeys/founder-detection.ts`

**Key Functions**:
- `detectFounderIntent()` - Analyzes conversation to detect founders
- `detectCompanyStage()` - Classifies company stage
- `detectPainPoints()` - Identifies current challenges
- `extractFounderContext()` - Extracts metrics (burn, runway, etc)

**Detection Logic**:
1. Keyword matching (fast, no API calls)
2. AI analysis with Claude (deep understanding)
3. Pattern recognition (multiple pain points = founder)
4. Context extraction (metrics, stage, urgency)

---

### Founder Flows
**File**: `/lib/journeys/founder-flows.ts`

**Key Functions**:
- `getFounderActions()` - Recommends actions based on profile
- `startHiringFlow()` - Launch Wells Onyx search
- `startGTMFlow()` - Launch customer acquisition campaign
- `startProcurementFlow()` - Create RFQ for sourcing
- `startFundraisingFlow()` - Draft pitch deck and find investors

**Flow Orchestration**:
```typescript
// Example: Hiring a CFO
const result = await startHiringFlow({
  userId: 'user_123',
  role: 'CFO',
  companyName: 'FinanceOS',
  companyStage: 'series-a',
  requirements: 'Need CFO with fundraising experience'
})
// Returns: { success, message, searchId, nextSteps }
```
