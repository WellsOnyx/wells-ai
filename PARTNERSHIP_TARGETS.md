## 💰 Partnership Economics

### Revenue Share Model:
- Grain & Vault earns 0.33% on all transactions
- Partner earns 15-25% of Grain & Vault's revenue on referred volume
- Example: $10M monthly volume = $33K fees = $5K-8K partner payout

### Integration Tiers:

**1. Light:** Referral link + co-marketing (Wilshire Law Firm model)
- Partner promotes GRAIN to their users
- We track via referral codes
- Monthly payout based on volume

**2. Medium:** API integration + embedded payments (BigCommerce model)
- Partner embeds GRAIN as payment option
- Seamless UX for end users
- Higher volume, higher payouts

**3. Deep:** White-label treasury for partner's customers (Future model)
- Partner's users get GRAIN treasury accounts
- Branded as partner's product
- Highest volume, ongoing revenue share
