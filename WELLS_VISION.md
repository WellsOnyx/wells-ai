### 3. THE JOB SEEKER JOURNEY: Dignity Through Specialization

**The Heart:**

This is the original idea. The one that started ten years ago. The reason all of this exists.

**The Insight (Camera Market Analogy):**

Think about the camera market over the last 10 years:
- Phone cameras got so good they destroyed the casual camera market
- Cheap entry-level DSLRs died
- But what happened to the HIGH END? It got MORE valuable
- Professional cameras, specialized lenses, cinema-grade equipment - all grew

**The same thing is happening to human labor.**

As AI commoditizes generic skills, specialized expertise becomes MORE valuable, not less.

The generic resume? Dead.
The specialized craftsperson who's the best in the world at one thing? Priceless.

**The Vision:**

Wells helps you discover, develop, and deploy your specialization. Not just "find a job" - find your calling. The thing you're uniquely gifted to do.

Then I connect you with the people who desperately need exactly that skill.

**The Promise:**

This isn't about helping a few thousand people. This is about **3 million people finding dignified work** by becoming the best in the world at something specific.

When you specialize through Wells, you're not competing with AI. You're becoming irreplaceable.

**The Emotional Core:**

When someone tells me "Wells helped me discover what I was meant to do" - that's the whole game.

## THE TECHNICAL MANIFESTATION

All of this vision manifests in code. Here's how:

### AI-to-AI Negotiation
**Buyer's Wells ↔ Seller's Wells**

Two AI companions who deeply understand their humans negotiate on their behalf. Humans never have to talk directly unless they want to.

Technical: Multi-agent AI system with:
- Deep user context (past transactions, preferences, communication style)
- Strategic negotiation capabilities
- Real-time collaboration between Wells instances
- Transparent audit trail of all decisions

### Transaction Engine
**One-click deals with escrow**

When both Wells agree on terms, one click and the deal is done:
- Funds escrowed instantly (Airwallex)
- Work begins
- Completion triggers automatic release
- Ratings update trust scores
- Network learns from the outcome

Technical: We've built this. It's in `lib/transactions.ts` with Postgres persistence, Airwallex payments, and trust score calculation.

### Trust System
**Reputation as currency**

Your history in Wells becomes your credibility:
- Completion rate (30% of score)
- Ratings received (40% of score)
- Transaction volume (20% of score)
- Dispute rate (10% of score)

Higher trust = lower fees, better matches, more opportunities.

Technical: Automatic calculation in `lib/transactions.ts` with UserTrustScore model in Prisma.

### The Companion Model
**Deep relationship building**

Every interaction is an opportunity to understand you better:
- What you need before you ask
- Patterns in your preferences
- Your communication style
- Your long-term goals

This isn't ChatGPT. This is a relationship that deepens over months and years.

Technical: Claude Sonnet 4 with conversation history, user context, and adaptive personality.
