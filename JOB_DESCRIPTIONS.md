## About Wells Onyx

Wells Onyx is building the AI-native operating system for professional services, partnerships, and back-office automation. We operate on a 33 basis points (0.33%) revenue model—we only make money when our clients close deals. 10% of our fees flow to Adullam, our 501(c)(3) mission supporting education and opportunity.

We're not a typical startup. We're a force multiplier: one person + AI = what used to take 50 people.
