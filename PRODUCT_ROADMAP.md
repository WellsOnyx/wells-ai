## 🎨 DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Reference these when evaluating ideas:

1. **Relationships over transactions** - Does this deepen bonds?
2. **Dignity through specialization** - Does this help people find their genius?
3. **Abundance over scarcity** - Does this grow the pie?
4. **Meet them where they are** - Does this start simple and expand organically?
5. **Do no harm** - Does this align with flourishing?
6. **AI-to-AI is human-to-human** - Does this make negotiations better?
7. **Trust compounds** - Does this build reputation capital?
8. **The prophecy matters** - Could this lead to marriages?
