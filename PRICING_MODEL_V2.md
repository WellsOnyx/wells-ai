# Wells AI Pricing Model V2 - Success-Based Seller Fee

**Effective Date:** Immediate
**Model:** 10% success fee (seller pays, buyer pays asking price)

---

## Core Pricing

### Base Fee Structure
- **Fee:** 10% of transaction value
- **Who Pays:** Seller
- **When:** Success only (no upfront cost, no monthly fees)
- **Buyer Experience:** 0% cost (pays asking price only)

### Why This Works

**For Buyers:**
- Zero cost to use Wells AI
- Access to pre-vetted suppliers
- Faster procurement cycles
- No sales pressure (we're not taking their money)

**For Sellers:**
- Only pay when we deliver a deal
- No upfront marketing spend
- Pre-qualified buyers (high close rate)
- Automated deal flow

**For Wells AI:**
- Aligned incentives (we only make money when you make money)
- Higher margin than 5.5% buyer fee
- Sustainable business model
- Scales with transaction volume

## Objection Handling Framework

When sellers balk at 10%, we have 5 proven responses:

### Response 1: Show the Value (ROI Calculator)

**Seller says:** "10% is too high"

**Wells AI responds:**
```
I understand. Let's compare the real economics:

Traditional Sales (without Wells AI):
├─ Sales team salary: $120K/year
├─ Marketing spend: $50K/year
├─ Trade shows: $30K/year
├─ Close rate: 5-10%
├─ Time to close: 3-6 months
└─ Cost per deal: $20K-50K+

Wells AI (with 10% fee):
├─ Sales team: $0
├─ Marketing: $0
├─ Trade shows: $0
├─ Close rate: 60-80% (pre-qualified)
├─ Time to close: Days to weeks
└─ Cost per deal: 10% only on closed deals

On a $500K deal:

- Your net: $450K
- Deals you wouldn't have had: Priceless

Plus:
✓ No monthly fees
✓ No upfront costs
✓ Pay only on success
✓ Unlimited deal flow potential
```

### Response 2: Buyer Absorbs Fee (If Buyer Is Motivated)

**Seller says:** "I can't afford 10%"

**Wells AI to Buyer:**
```

Would you be willing to absorb the fee to make this deal happen?

Your options:
1. Pay asking price: $500K (seller pays 10% = $50K to Wells AI)
2. Absorb fee: Pay $550K (seller gets full $500K, you pay Wells AI $50K)

Why you might choose option 2:
- Deal closes immediately (no negotiation delay)
- Seller is happier (gets full asking price)
- You still save vs. alternatives:
  ├─ Traditional procurement: 2-4 months
  ├─ Broker fees: 8-15%
  ├─ Risk of wrong supplier: Priceless
  └─ Your time saved: Worth it

Fair trade for speed and certainty?
```

### Response 3: Split the Fee (Compromise Path)

**Both parties hesitate**

**Wells AI proposes:**
```

├─ Buyer pays: $525K (asking price + 5%)
├─ Seller receives: $475K (asking price - 5%)

Why this works:
✓ Buyer: Still better than traditional channels
✓ Seller: Only 5% instead of 10%
✓ Both: Deal closes faster than any alternative
✓ Fair: Both parties contribute to the value created

Sound fair?
```

## Pricing Tiers (Official)

### Tier 1: Standard
- **Fee:** 10% success only
- **Who:** All transactions
- **Benefits:**
  - Zero upfront cost
  - Pre-qualified buyers
  - Automated deal flow

### Tier 2: Volume Partner
- **Fee:** 5% (first deal), 7.5% (deals 2-5), 10% (thereafter)
- **Qualification:** Commit to 5+ deals in 12 months
- **Benefits:**
  - All Standard benefits
  - Priority placement
  - Dedicated support
  - Custom API integration

### Tier 3: Strategic Partner
- **Fee:** Custom (based on volume)
- **Qualification:** $5M+ annual transaction volume
- **Benefits:**
  - All Volume Partner benefits
  - White-label options
  - Revenue share models
  - Co-marketing opportunities
  - Early Grain partner (0% fees from day 1)

## Fee Flexibility Matrix

When to flex on the 10% fee:

| Situation | Recommended Action | Fee Adjustment |
|-----------|-------------------|----------------|
| First-time seller, hesitant | Volume discount offer | 5% first deal |
| Buyer willing to absorb | Shift fee to buyer | Buyer pays 10% |
| Both parties balking | Split fee proposal | 5% each side |
| High-value strategic deal | Custom negotiation | 7-8% range |
| Seller has repeat potential | Volume partnership | Tiered 5/7.5/10% |
| Early Grain partner | Roadmap pitch | Lock in 0% future |
