## What is GRAIN?

GRAIN is a USDC-backed, auto-revaluing stablecoin for B2B settlements. Unlike traditional stablecoins that maintain a fixed 1:1 peg, GRAIN's price automatically appreciates based on the Net Asset Value (NAV) of the underlying Vault.

**Simple Formula:**
```
GRAIN Price = Total Vault NAV / Total GRAIN Supply
```

As the Vault's assets grow through treasury yields, investments, and transaction fees, GRAIN price increases automatically.

## Risks

### 1. Smart Contract Risk

**Risk:** Bug in Solidity code could lock funds.

**Mitigation:**
- Audited by Trail of Bits
- $100K bug bounty
- Insurance policy

### 2. NAV Decline Risk

**Risk:** Vault assets decline → GRAIN price drops.

**Mitigation:**
- 110% collateralization minimum
- Circuit breaker (auto-pause if NAV drops >10%)
- Diversified asset allocation

### 3. Regulatory Risk

**Risk:** SEC classifies GRAIN as security.

**Mitigation:**
- Legal opinion: GRAIN is utility token
- No "investment contract" (Howey Test)
- Can pivot to licensed structure if needed

### 4. Liquidity Risk

**Risk:** Can't sell GRAIN quickly.

**Mitigation:**
- Instant USDC redemption (72-hour time-lock for >$100K)
- Growing B2B network for spending GRAIN
- Future exchange listings

### 5. Market Risk

**Risk:** Stablecoin market collapse (USDC de-pegs).

**Mitigation:**
- Diversify to multiple stablecoins (USDT, DAI)
- Add fiat backing if needed
- Partner with licensed stablecoin issuers
